DEEP WORK

Deep Work is a popular self-improvement and productivity book written by Cal Newport and published in 2016.



Author → Cal Newport

Cal Newport is an American author and professor. He is best known for writing books about focus, productivity, and personal development.

About this book:

Deep Work teaches how to focus deeply without distractions.It explains how strong concentration can help people learn difficultthings, improve their skills, and become more productive.
