RICH DAD POOR DAD

Rich Dad Poor Dad is a popular personal finance and self-help book written by Robert T. Kiyosaki and Sharon Lechter and published in 1997.



Author → Robert T. Kiyosaki

Robert T. Kiyosaki is an American author and entrepreneur. He is best known for writing Rich Dad Poor Dad, a book that teaches important lessons about money, financial education, assets, and financial independence.

About this book:

Rich Dad Poor Dad explains the importance of financial education and understanding how money works. The book compares different attitudes toward money and teaches readers about assets, liabilities, saving, investing, and building financial independence.
