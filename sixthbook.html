THE ALMANACK OF NAVAL RAVIKANT

The Almanack of Naval Ravikant is a popular book about wealth and happiness, compiled by Eric Jorgenson and published in 2020.



Author → Eric Jorgenson

Eric Jorgenson is an American writer and entrepreneur. He compiled the ideas and wisdom of Naval Ravikant into The Almanack of Naval Ravikant.

About this book:

The Almanack of Naval Ravikant shares ideas about building wealth, making better decisions, developing useful skills, finding happiness, and living a meaningful life.
