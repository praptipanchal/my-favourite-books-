THANK YOU FOR LEAVING

Thank You for Leaving is an emotional poetry book written by Ritvik Singh.



Author → Ritvik Singh

Ritvik Singh is an Indian author known for writing emotional and relatable poetry about love, heartbreak, relationships, and healing.

About this book:

Thank You for Leaving explores love, heartbreak, memories, and the processof letting go. The book expresses the emotions of losing someone and findingthe strength to move forward.
